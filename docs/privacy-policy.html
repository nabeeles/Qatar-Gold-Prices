Privacy Policy — Qatar Gold Price Tracker

Effective Date: October 16, 2026
Application Name: Qatar Gold Price Tracker (com.qatargold.tracker)

1. Overview & Privacy-First Commitment

Qatar Gold Price Tracker ("the Application") is built on a Privacy-First, Data-Sovereign architecture. We do not require user accounts, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal identity, location, or gold portfolio holdings to any external server.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

2.1 Personal Gold Portfolio ("My Gold" Vault)

When you record gold purchases in the My Gold tab (such as asset labels, karat purity, weight in grams, purchase price per gram, and purchase date):

  • All portfolio records are stored exclusively on your device inside a sandboxed local SQLite database (vault.db).
  • No Cloud Transmission: Your portfolio data is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • No Automatic Cloud Backup: Android Auto Backup (android:allowBackup="false") is explicitly disabled so your local financial vault is not inadvertently backed up to unencrypted cloud storage.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete individual entries at any time within the app, or erase the entire local database by uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

3. Information Processed for Cloud Features

3.1 Live Market Gold Prices

To deliver hourly Qatari retail gold rates (24K, 22K, 21K, and 18K) and historical market trends:

  • The Application connects to our read-only market database hosted on Supabase over encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Access is authenticated using an ephemeral, anonymous session ID generated by Supabase Auth (signInAnonymously). This anonymous identifier is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or device hardware serial numbers.

3.2 Optional Push Notification Price Alerts

If you choose to create a custom gold price alert in the Alerts tab:

  • With your explicit permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), the Application generates an Expo Push Token (ExponentPushToken[...]) via Expo Notification Services / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • We store only your anonymous session identifier (user_id), your device's Expo Push Token (expo_push_token), and the alert parameters you selected (karat, target_price, and condition).
  • Purpose: This data is used solely to send you a push notification when the Qatari retail gold market crosses your target price, after which the alert is automatically deactivated.
  • Deletion & Opt-Out: You can delete any active alert directly inside the Alerts tab of the Application (which permanently removes the record from our database via Row-Level Security), or revoke notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Permissions Requested by the Application

Permission Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Required to fetch live Qatari gold market rates and historical trend data over HTTPS.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional; requested only when you create a price alert so we can notify you when your target gold price is reached.
android.permission.VIBRATE Used for tactile feedback on alert notifications.

The Application explicitly blocks access to your camera, microphone (RECORD_AUDIO), photos/media/files (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_*), contacts, and location.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: The Application contains zero third-party advertising networks, behavioral analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share any user or device data with third parties.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Supabase (encrypted public gold market price database and anonymous push-alert subscription table) and Expo / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notification delivery).

6. Market Information Disclaimer

Qatar Gold Price Tracker is an informational price comparison and personal portfolio calculation utility. Displayed gold prices are aggregated from publicly listed Qatari jewelry retailers and currency exchanges for reference only. They do not constitute financial, investment, legal, or trading advice. Retail making charges and store-specific terms may vary.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is a general-audience financial reference utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect architectural or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
